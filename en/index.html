Frequently Asked Questions

How much data can a QR code hold?
A QR code can hold up to about 4,296 alphanumeric characters or 1,817 kanji characters. Keep content short for best scan reliability.
What does error correction level mean?
Error correction determines how much of the QR code can be damaged while remaining readable. L = 7%, M = 15%, Q = 25%, H = 30%. Higher levels make denser but more durable codes.
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. QR codes are generated entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
What download formats are available?
You can download as PNG (raster image) or SVG (vector format). SVG can be scaled to any size without quality loss.
How many QR codes can I generate in batch mode?
Batch mode supports up to 50 QR codes at once. Enter one text or URL per line.
What CSV format should I use?
Use name,content as the first row. The name becomes the download filename and content becomes the QR Code data. Batch data can also be downloaded in the same CSV format.
